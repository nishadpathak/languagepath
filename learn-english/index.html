Learn English for work in the Gulf

Last updated 15 October 2026

Free, practical English for your first months working in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and the rest of the Gulf. Choose your language: every page has 35 everyday sentences with the meaning in your language and the English pronunciation written in your own script.

  • English for Hindi speakers — हिन्दी
    अंग्रेज़ी सीखिए — काम और रोज़मर्रा की ज़िंदगी के लिए।
  • English for Urdu speakers — اردو
    انگریزی سیکھیں — کام اور روزمرہ زندگی کے لیے۔
  • English for Tagalog speakers — Tagalog
    Matuto ng Ingles para sa trabaho at araw-araw na buhay.
  • English for Bengali speakers — বাংলা
    ইংরেজি শিখুন — কাজ আর প্রতিদিনের জীবনের জন্য।
  • English for Arabic speakers — العربية
    تعلّم الإنجليزية للعمل والحياة اليومية.

Topics on every page: at work, housing, at the bank, at the clinic, getting around, salary, at the visa office. Each page also has tips for speaking English at work, asking people to repeat, and handling numbers and documents.
