English for Gulf workers — for Bengali speakers

Last updated 15 October 2026

ইংরেজি শিখুন — কাজ আর প্রতিদিনের জীবনের জন্য।

These are the English sentences people use most in their first months working in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman. Each one has the meaning in Bengali and a simple way to say the English, written in Bengali script so you can read it straight away.

English is the shared language of many Gulf workplaces, shops, banks and clinics, so a few clear sentences make daily life easier. Learn five sentences a day: read the meaning, say the English out loud using the pronunciation column, then cover the English and try to say it from memory.

Tips for speaking English at work

Start polite, keep it short

In Gulf workplaces, short and polite English works best. Start with “Good morning” or “Excuse me”, say what you need in one sentence, and finish with “Thank you”. “Please” makes any request softer. You do not need perfect grammar: “AC not working, please fix” is understood, but the full sentences below sound more confident and get faster help.

Ask them to repeat

If you do not understand, it is normal to ask. Say “Sorry, can you repeat, please?” or “Please speak slowly.” Asking is better than guessing, especially about money, documents and safety instructions.

Numbers, dates and money

Practise saying your phone number, your room number and amounts of money out loud. When you hear a date or an amount, repeat it back: “The 25th? 1,500 dirhams?” This avoids mistakes with rent, salary and appointments.

Documents

Always keep copies or photos of your passport, visa, ID card, contract and payslips. At an office, show the document and use the phrases below. If something is unclear, ask for it in writing: “Can you write it down, please?”

Health and safety

At a clinic, say where it hurts and since when: “My back hurts since yesterday.” At work, ask for safety equipment if you do not have it, and ask to be shown how to use a new machine before you start.
