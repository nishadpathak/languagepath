About LanguagePath

Figure it out. Say it. Remember it.

LanguagePath is a free website for learning languages by constructing sentences rather than memorising them. It covers 69 languages, with travel phrasebooks for the cities people visit most.

Who runs LanguagePath

LanguagePath.org is owned and operated by NMP DMCC, a company registered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. It was founded by Nishad Pathak, who started it after years of moving between countries and wanting a way to learn the essentials of a language by understanding how sentences are built, instead of by drilling lists of phrases.

You can reach us at any time through our contact page.

Our mission

We want anyone, anywhere, to be able to start speaking a new language within minutes — for free, without creating an account, and without handing over personal data. That means the courses run in your browser, your progress stays on your own device, and every lesson is designed to get you saying real sentences out loud as early as possible.

The method

Most courses make you memorise phrases. LanguagePath assumes you can reason them out. You learn a few small pieces, then assemble real sentences yourself, say them aloud, and only then reveal and check the answer. Because you built them, you remember them — and spaced review brings them back just before you would forget.

The core loop: Understand → Construct → Say → Hear → Rebuild.

Editorial approach

  • Written from course data, not scraped. Every lesson, phrase list, pronunciation note, culture tip and written guide on the site is produced by our team from our own course material. We do not copy content from other websites.
  • Pronunciation is approximate. Respellings such as “GRAH-see-ahs” are guides for English speakers; the audio and native speakers are the reference.
  • Review is ongoing. Our flagship courses (Mandarin, Arabic, French, German, Portuguese, Spanish, Turkish, Telugu and Farsi) are the most complete. Native-speaker review of every course is ongoing, and pages that have not yet been reviewed say so. When readers report an error, we fix it and note the correction.
  • No invented facts. Our travel and culture notes stick to well-established, practical information. We avoid statistics and claims that date quickly.
  • Corrections. Found a mistake? Please tell us — include the page address and what should change.

How LanguagePath is funded

The site is free. It is supported by advertising (Google AdSense), which loads only on content pages and only after you consent, and by clearly labelled travel partner links (for example flight searches) on travel pages, which may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. Advertisers have no influence over our lessons or guides. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
