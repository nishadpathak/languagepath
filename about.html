About LanguagePath

Figure it out. Say it. Remember it.

LanguagePath teaches you to construct a language, not memorize it. Instead of flashcards, the teacher hands you a piece, asks you to build something, you work it out — say it out loud — then reveal and check. Every step reuses what you already built.

The method

Most courses make you memorize phrases. LanguagePath assumes you're smart enough to reason them out. You learn the small pieces, then assemble real sentences yourself. Because you built them, you own them — and they come back in spaced recall right before you'd forget.

The core loop: Reason → Build → Speak → Listen → Recall → Converse.

The feature underneath

Backed by genuine learning tools — graduated lessons, spaced-repetition recall with speech checks, listening drills, shadowing, real dialogues, quizzes, culture notes, pocket phrases and pronunciation. The Build feature generates brand-new sentences from the vocabulary and structures you've already learned, so practice never feels repetitive. Explore also has a clickable languages map and Path — construct a sentence in each city’s language and beat the clock.

No account. No tracking. Free.

LanguagePath has no accounts and no sign-up, keeps your progress only in your own browser, and keeps your data private. Advertising only loads after you consent — and the site stays fully usable if you reject it. Learn privately.
