LanguagePath Pro FAQ

Last updated: 20 October 2026

LanguagePath has no accounts. Pro and phrase packs are unlocked with a licence key that is saved in your browser.

How does Pro get activated after checkout?

After you pay on Stripe's checkout page, Stripe sends you back to our thank-you page. That page checks with our server until Stripe confirms the payment (usually a few seconds), then shows your licence key (and download links for packs) and saves the key in this browser automatically. Pro is then active on this browser.

Copy and keep your licence key — it is how you restore Pro later.

How do I restore Pro?

If you cleared your browser data or Pro no longer shows as active, open Restore purchase and paste your licence key. Our server checks it against your purchase record and saves it in the browser again. Pack buyers also get fresh download links there (download links expire after 48 hours).

Lost your key? Email languagepathorg@gmail.com from the address you used at checkout, ideally with your Stripe receipt.

How do I use Pro on another device?

On the new device or browser, open Restore purchase and paste the same licence key. Lesson progress is stored separately in each browser, so to bring your progress too, use Export progress in the Hub tab on the old device and Import progress on the new one.

What happens when my subscription renews or ends?

Pro renews automatically ($4.99/month or $29/year) until cancelled. Your browser re-checks the key regularly: renewals extend it, and once a cancelled subscription's paid period is over, Pro switches off. Packs you bought stay yours.

How do I cancel?

Email languagepathorg@gmail.com from your checkout email address (or include your licence key). We cancel the subscription in Stripe so it won't renew. See the Terms for cancellation, refunds and pack access.
