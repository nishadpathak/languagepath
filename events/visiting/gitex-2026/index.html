Say your first words at the stand

A three-minute Arabic practice for the show floor: introduce yourself, ask for a demo and arrange a follow-up.

Start booth practice

Summit: 7 December 2026, Dubai World Trade Centre.
Expo: 8–11 December 2026, Dubai Exhibition Centre, Expo City Dubai.

Check dates and venues on the organizer’s site.

One exhibitor stand. Four lines.

Read the English cue, try saying it out loud, then reveal the Arabic. These are polite Modern Standard Arabic phrases; the pronunciation guides are approximate.

  1. 01 Introduce yourself

    Hello, my name is … and I’m from … company.

    Reveal the introduction

    مرحبًا، اسمي … وأنا من شركة …

    Marḥaban, ismī … wa-anā min sharikat …

  2. 02 Ask for a demo

    May I see a demo?

    Reveal the demo question

    هل يمكنني مشاهدة عرض توضيحي؟

    Hal yumkinunī mushāhadat ʿarḍ tawḍīḥī?

  3. 03 Exchange details

    Can we exchange contact information?

    Reveal the contact question

    هل يمكننا تبادل معلومات التواصل؟

    Hal yumkinunā tabādul maʿlūmāt at-tawāṣul?

  4. 04 Set up the follow-up

    Thank you. Could you email me the details?

    Reveal the follow-up

    شكرًا. هل يمكنكم إرسال التفاصيل إليّ بالبريد الإلكتروني؟

    Shukran. Hal yumkinukum irsāl at-tafāṣīl ilayya bil-barīd al-iliktrūnī?

After the stand visit

Send a short note while the conversation is fresh: “Thanks for the demo at GITEX. Could you send the product sheet and suggest a time for a short follow-up call?”
